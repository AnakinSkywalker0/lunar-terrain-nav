# Expanded terrain matching: protocol v2

22 October 2026. Written before expanded-run outcomes. Exploratory evaluation on
five areas within the same Site04 DEM, not five independent lunar datasets.
The original v1 outcome is known. No matcher/guidance thresholds or gains change.

## Frozen matrix

Five areas x two altitudes (500, 2000 m) x three map conditions x three initial
horizontal errors x three noise seeds (11,22,33). Each configuration has one
stationary scan and two descents, matching correction OFF and ON. Total: 270
stationary scans, 540 descents, 270 paired comparisons.

Errors (east,north metres): e0=(+60,-40), e1=(-80,+50), e2=(+100,+80). Noise seed
and error are crossed factors, not substitutes for each other. Initial velocity
is (0,-10,0) m/s. Both arms get the same map, sensors and random-number schedule.

Conditions remain aligned, truth-only 12 m Gaussian relief change centred at
(+120,+60) m with 60 m sigma, and onboard map coordinate shift (+40,-24) m.
The shifted-map/e2 case may require a correction outside the fixed ±128 m search
range. This is a planned stress condition, not a reason to enlarge the search.

## Area selection before outcomes

Retain the original centre (6198,8512) in tile metres. For four additional areas,
use the generated 2049x2049 RAW elevation grid. Compute central-difference slope
at its 7.8125 m spacing. Eligible targets have maximum slope <5 degrees over a
7x7 neighbourhood, and lie >=1400 m from the tile boundary. Greedily select the
eligible point with greatest minimum Euclidean distance from previously selected
centres, breaking ties in row-major order. No matcher outputs guide selection.

Exact selected coordinates and target slopes are in
`Assets/Experiments/ExpandedSites.json`, generated by
`tools/plan_expanded_terrain_tests.py`. Four quadrant-centre searches were first
considered; the southeast search contained no qualifying flat target, so the
above uniform full-tile rule replaced it before any expanded simulation.

The 2.4 km ground/map crops are independent representations. Each area's
condition-specific terrain buffers are cached and reused across resets; vehicle
and estimator state, fuel, random generator and physics objects reset per run.

## Fixed algorithm and sensor model

`TerrainNavigationCore.cs` must match the SHA-256 of the v1 frozen source.
Keep v1 dynamics, estimator, guidance, range/noise/dropout, scan schedule,
matcher search and acceptance settings. This remains a simplified 3-DOF vehicle
with ideal attitude response and synthetic sensors; no ML training is run.

One evaluation improvement is explicit: v2 records actual PhysX TerrainCollider
contact and collision relative velocity through `TerrainTouchdownRecorder`.
V1 stopped at an 8 cm proximity threshold, which can be misleading on sloping
ground. Contact-only data never enter guidance or matching. Compare OFF/ON
within v2; do not treat differences from v1 as a new algorithm improvement.

Success requires contact, vertical impact speed <2 m/s, horizontal speed <1 m/s,
and target miss <10 m. Timeout is 360 simulated seconds. Retain timeouts, rejected
matches and confident wrong matches (>10 m proposed absolute error), including
bad performance at individual sites. Report per site, altitude, error and map
condition as well as pooled values. Report medians and upper-tail errors and
paired changes. Three seeds at a site do not constitute three independent sites.

## Execution and preservation

Before the full matrix, smoke-check actual contact and routing at the original
area and area01, 500 m, aligned map, e0, seed11 (six cases). Do not tune the
matcher on smoke outcomes. Save both smoke and full data separately. Save code
snapshots, source/input hashes, the site manifest, protocol, per-run trajectories,
sparse point clouds and scan diagnostics. Stop on infrastructure errors and
retain incomplete outputs. New data use `latest_expanded.txt`; v1's `latest.txt`
and original replay stay available. Results must not be selected by appearance.
